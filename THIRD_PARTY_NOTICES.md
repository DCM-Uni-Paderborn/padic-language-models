# Third-party materials

Original manuscript and project code are provided by Thomas Kühne. No new blanket license is assigned to those materials in this package. The notices below apply to the identified third-party data and do not replace their original terms.

- **WikiText** is supplied by Salesforce from Wikipedia articles. The frozen token sequences are numerical encodings of unchanged excerpts. Article titles, boundaries and text hashes are retained in the data manifests. The included material follows the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license declared by the [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext). The license text is in `licenses/wikitext-cc-by-sa-3.0.txt`. The card also lists GFDL as an alternative license.
- **CLINC150** is the dataset of Larson and colleagues, described in [An Evaluation Dataset for Intent Classification and Out-of-Scope Prediction](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1131/). Original texts, selected-row metadata and token encodings are included under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. The [source repository](https://github.com/clinc/oos-eval) and exact source version are recorded in the input manifests. The license is in `licenses/clinc150-cc-by-3.0.txt`. Tokenization and the experimental domain/intent selection are transformations documented by the code and manifests.
- **PG-19** is Google DeepMind's [language-modelling benchmark](https://github.com/google-deepmind/pg19), distributed under Apache 2.0. This package contains selected token prefixes and original source metadata, rather than a duplicate whole-book corpus. Numerical tokenization and prefix selection are the documented transformations. Book titles, authors where present, source URLs and checksums remain in the input manifests. The license is in `licenses/pg19-apache-2.0.txt`.
- **SmolLM2** checkpoints are obtained from Hugging Face at the recorded versions. The [135M](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M) and [360M](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-360M) model cards specify Apache 2.0. Full checkpoint weights are not mirrored here.
- Related public implementations are cited and identified in the source-version manifests. Their code was inspected for the paper's comparisons and is not incorporated into the project's independent learner.
