## Endpoints, allowance and decision rule

Primary endpoints are paired token-weighted DeltaNLL over all scored next-token targets and over affected targets128..2046 separately. For each article preserve candidate/reference loss sums and identical target counts. Stock attention, fixed uniform+recent coverage, and the same-seed ordinary coarsened binary hierarchy are the three required comparators. Uniform is the strongest fixed sparse model-loss control in the development gate; coarsening is the strongest finite hierarchy in every long-context seed. Keeping both prevents comparison only with a weak baseline. Resource payload differences must remain visible.

The quality allowance is `delta=log(1.01)=0.009950330853168092` nats/token, a one-percent relative PPL allowance. This prospectively adopts the earlier routing-specific proposal as a prototype quality tolerance. It is justified here only as a screening allowance for a substantial128/2048 logical-position reduction in one altered layer; it is not a community standard, a hardware saving, a guarantee of useful model behavior or justification for a whole-model deployment. Requiring the affected-target endpoint as well prevents unchanged early targets alone from satisfying it. A method with greater state or lower speed can pass this quality gate without being a competitive resource/quality operating point.

Freeze20,000 paired document bootstrap resamples, NumPy `PCG64` seed514203, samplingN article indices with replacement per resample. Each statistic recomputes total paired loss divided by total scored targets; no independent token or head resampling. Use the upper97.5th percentile by NumPy linear quantile interpolation as the one-sided upper bound. Report every bound, including failed/inconclusive comparisons, raw point estimates and per-article values. Bootstrap coverage is an approximation and must be stated as such.

Binary and ternary are the two primary candidate families. A family receives the limited label "quality non-inferior in the frozen three-seed first-layer study" only ifN>=40 and every one of its three fixed seeds passes the upper-bound<delta criterion against all three comparators for both all-target and affected-target endpoints. This is an intersection requirement, not best-seed selection or seed-population inference. Each family's union null requires all constituent comparisons to pass; the97.5% per-comparison level and two candidate families give a conservative nominal5% familywise screening allocation under valid per-comparison bounds. Do not claim exact finite-sample error control for bootstrap bounds or inference over all possible training seeds. Failure/inconclusiveness does not establish inferiority.

The matched-partition hierarchy/grid differences, all remaining readouts, projected/value errors, mass and overlap are secondary estimates. Report two-sided2.5/97.5% paired-document percentile intervals for secondary DeltaNLL without a new superiority claim or a favorable multiple-comparison selection. Primary non-inferiority does not imply equality, superiority, a p-adic-specific mechanism or a native efficiency advantage.
