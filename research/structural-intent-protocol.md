## Representation and matched learning

Use unchanged SmolLM2-360M revision `f8027fd0eaeea54caa13c31d31b9fdc459c38b49`, BF16 eager, state SHA256 `b9fa643b37b587b20e70991f30a7e24a9897aeaa159467fce79b59152f70d583`. Tokenize only utterance text, without label names or target paths, at a fixed maximum64 tokens with right padding. Record truncations. Mean the final hidden states over nonpadding tokens in FP32. Fit a64-coordinate PCA/whitening transform exclusively on adaptation states; all models receive these identical features and labels. The LLM is never fine-tuned and this classification probe is not a generative token-NLL intervention.

A finite binary address has four domain digits followed by four within-domain intent digits in low-digit-first order. At depth4 it identifies one of ten domains; depth8 identifies an intent. Only occupied seen-intent leaves are fitted. Omitting unary branches yields139 trainable binary decisions for140 leaves. All heads and probabilities are real. Prefix conditional probabilities multiply along a leaf path. Their domain-prefix probability equals the sum over that domain's leaves. This is ordinary hierarchical softmax expressed with a finite p-adic address; its equal-tree control is a mathematical/numerical identity, not a rival claim of unique p-adic quality.

Fit every declared method at seeds17,29,43:

- `semantic_prefix`: the fixed domain/intent prefix hierarchy,139 affine binary heads.
- `shuffled_prefix`: identical tree topology and parameter count, but permute140 semantic labels across occupied leaf addresses using RNG seed+1000. It receives the same intent AND true-domain supervision.
- `flat_joint`:140-class softmax with one reference logit fixed to zero,139 free affine heads. True-domain probabilities aggregate the corresponding leaves; it receives the same hierarchy labels and joint loss.
- `direct_domain`: ten-class softmax with one reference logit fixed to zero, nine free heads. This strong coarse-task control has fewer parameters and no fine task.

The first three classifiers have9035 learned real parameters each; direct-domain has585. Common encoder costs are separate. Initialize coefficients from the same seeded139x64 Gaussian array with standard deviation0.01 and zero biases; direct-domain uses its first nine rows. Train with400 full-batch FP64 Adam steps, learning rate0.03, betas0.9/0.999, epsilon1e-8, no schedule/early stopping/tuning. The joint objective is mean intent cross-entropy plus mean true-domain cross-entropy plus0.0005 times coefficient squared norm. Direct-domain uses only its domain cross-entropy and the same coefficient penalty. All fits, initial/final states and every25-step objective are preserved. This is real optimization, not a p-adic derivative.

## Four properties and their limits

Exhaustive small arithmetic/codec fixtures check quotient-compatible addition/multiplication for primes2,3,5 and prefix invariance under multiples of p^k. Independent prefix-tree traversal checks learned probabilities and domain aggregation. Archive60 exact branch-score certificates for the first20 unseen test examples in each primary seed: the recorded domain-path contributions reproduce its predicted domain probability. These certify the classifier computation and displayed hierarchy, not causal explanations of the underlying LLM or robustness to real embedding noise. Progressive probability aggregation is also available to a flat classifier; exact contracts are not automatically a comparative accuracy advantage. Only the unseen-intent experiment tests practical semantic generalization.
